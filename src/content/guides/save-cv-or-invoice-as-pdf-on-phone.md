A CV or invoice should be sent as a **PDF**, because a PDF looks the same on every phone and computer. Our [CV Builder](/cv-builder) and [Invoice Generator](/invoice-generator) make the document on your screen. To turn it into a PDF file, you use your phone's **print** screen, which has a "save as PDF" option. It looks odd the first time, so here are the steps.

## Why you "print" to get a PDF

The tools use your browser's own print feature, which creates a real PDF with text you can select and search. That is better for job applications, because employers' screening software can read it. The same print screen also lets you save instead of printing on paper. Nothing is uploaded: the PDF is created on your phone.

## Before you start

- Fill in the form and check the preview. What you see in the preview is what will be in the PDF.
- Your details are **not saved**. If you close the page before saving the PDF, you start again.

## On an Android phone (Chrome)

1. Press **Print or save as PDF** under the preview.
2. The print screen opens. At the top, where it says **Destination** or **Printer**, choose **Save as PDF**.
3. Check that the paper size is **A4**.
4. Tap the round **PDF** or **Download** button.
5. Choose a name and tap **Save**. The file goes to your **Downloads** folder.

## On an iPhone (Safari)

1. Press **Print or save as PDF** under the preview.
2. A print options screen opens with a small preview of the page.
3. **Pinch outward** on that small preview with two fingers. It opens as a full-size PDF.
4. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
5. Choose **Save to Files**, pick a folder, and tap **Save**.

Screens differ slightly between phone models and software versions, so if you do not see a button exactly as described, look for the closest option with the words *PDF*, *Share* or *Save*.

## On a computer

1. Press **Print or save as PDF**.
2. In the print window, set **Destination** to **Save as PDF**.
3. If you see the web address or the date printed at the top or bottom of the page, turn off **Headers and footers** in the options.
4. Click **Save**.

## Check the PDF before you send it

Open the saved file and look at it:

- Is everything on the page, with nothing cut off?
- Is the text readable, and the spelling of your name and phone number correct?
- For an invoice, do the totals add up, and are the bank details right?

## Fixing common problems

**The print screen does not open.** Make sure you are using your normal browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone) and not a browser built into another app, such as one inside a social app. Those often block printing.

**The PDF has the wrong paper size.** Choose **A4** in the print options before saving.

**The colours are missing.** Look for an option such as **Background graphics** and turn it on.

**It is on two pages and you wanted one.** In the form, shorten long text, remove empty entries, or use fewer bullet points.

**You closed the page and lost everything.** The tools do not store your details, to protect your privacy. Keep a copy of your text in your notes app so you can paste it back in later.

## Common questions

**Is a PDF better than a Word file?** For sending to employers or customers, yes. A PDF keeps the same layout everywhere, and people cannot change it by accident.

**Will employers' software be able to read it?** Yes. The text in the PDF is real text, not a picture, and the layout is a simple single column.

**Can I edit the PDF later?** Not easily. Go back to the tool, change the form, and save a new PDF.
