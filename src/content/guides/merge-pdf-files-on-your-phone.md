Job portals, school applications and offices often want **one PDF** that holds everything: a certificate, an ID, a letter and a form. If your documents are separate files, you need to join them. You can do that on your phone in a browser, without installing an app and without sending your documents to anyone.

## The short answer

1. Open the [Merge PDF tool](/merge-pdf).
2. Tap the box and choose all the PDFs you want to join.
3. Put them in the right order with the **↑** and **↓** buttons.
4. Tap **Merge PDFs**, then **Download**.

## Step by step

### 1. Get your files ready

Make sure each document is already a PDF and is saved on your phone. Photos of documents are not PDFs yet. If you only have photos, convert them to PDF first with your phone's scanning feature, then come back.

It helps to give your files names that sort in the order you want, for example "1 Letter", "2 Certificate", "3 ID".

### 2. Add the files

Open the tool, tap the box, and select your PDFs. You can choose several at once, and you can add more later by tapping the box again. The tool accepts up to **30 files** at a time, **100 MB** each, and **300 MB** in total.

### 3. Set the order

The files are listed with numbers. Use **↑** and **↓** next to a file to move it. Use **Remove** if you added one by mistake. The order on this list is the order of the pages in the final PDF.

### 4. Merge and download

Tap **Merge PDFs**. When it finishes, you see the number of pages and the size of the new file. Tap **Download**. On most phones it is saved in your **Downloads** folder as `merged.pdf`.

### 5. Check the result

Open the merged file and scroll through it. Check that the pages are in the right order and that none are missing. The page count shown by the tool should equal the total pages of your separate files added together.

## Fixing common problems

**"This PDF is password-protected."** A locked PDF cannot be merged. Open it in the app you normally use, remove the password, save a copy, and add that copy instead.

**"This PDF could not be read. It may be damaged."** The file may be incomplete, for example from a download that stopped halfway. Download or save the file again and retry.

**The merged file is too big for the portal.** Shrink it with the [Compress PDF tool](/compress-pdf), or remove pages you do not need with the [Split PDF tool](/split-pdf).

**A page is the wrong way round.** The tool joins pages exactly as they are, so a sideways page stays sideways. Rotate it in your phone's PDF viewer before merging.

**You only need some pages from a file.** Use the [Split PDF tool](/split-pdf) first to extract just those pages, then merge.

## Is it safe to use for private documents?

The files are joined inside your own browser, on your phone. They are not uploaded to our servers, so we never see them. That matters for documents such as IDs and certificates. You can read the details on our [Privacy page](/privacy).

## Common questions

**Does merging reduce quality?** No. The pages are copied as they are, without being compressed again.

**Can I merge on an iPhone?** Yes. Use Safari, tap the box, and choose files from the **Files** app. If you do not see your PDF, check that it is saved in Files and not only inside another app.

**How do I find the file afterwards?** Open the **Files** app (on iPhone) or **Files** or **My Files** (on Android) and look in **Downloads**.
